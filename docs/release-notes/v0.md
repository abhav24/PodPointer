The first release of PodPointer. Point your AirPods case at the screen and tap it to control your slides or your Mac.

**To install:** download `PodPointer-v0.dmg`, open it, and drag PodPointer to Applications. PodPointer isn't notarized by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time. To open it, go to **System Settings > Privacy & Security** and select **Open Anyway**.

**What's included:**
- Presenter mode: tap for next slide, double-tap for previous slide, and aim a laser dot
- Mouse mode: rotate to move the cursor, tap to click, and double-tap to double-click
- Guided setup that calibrates for your grip and tests that everything works

**Before you start:**
- You'll need macOS 14 or later and AirPods that support head tracking, such as AirPods Pro.
- The case lid has to stay open, and Automatic Ear Detection has to be off.
- This build has been tested on one pair of AirPods Pro 2 with an Apple silicon Mac. Other setups are untested.

Results from your AirPods help a lot. See the [test plan](https://github.com/abhav24/PodPointer/blob/main/docs/TESTING.md).
